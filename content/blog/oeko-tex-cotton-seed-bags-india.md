OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified cotton seed bags from India are the standard specification for seed companies supplying UK garden retail chains, US organic farm supply, and any brand that wants to make a verified safety claim about its packaging. This guide covers what OEKO-TEX certification means in practice, why it matters for seed packaging specifically, how to verify a supplier's certificate, and what to specify when placing a wholesale order from India.

---

## What OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Certifies

OEKO-TEX Standard 100 is an independent testing and certification system for textile products. The certification confirms that the finished product has been tested and found free from harmful substances - including pesticide residues, heavy metals (lead, cadmium, chromium), formaldehyde, prohibited azo dyes, phthalates, and other chemicals - at concentrations below the thresholds set by the standard.

For a cotton seed bag, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 tells a buyer: the finished bag, as sold, has been tested by an accredited testing institute and does not contain harmful substances at detectable levels.

**What OEKO-TEX does NOT certify:**
- That the cotton was grown organically (that is GOTS's role)
- That production was environmentally sustainable
- That workers were treated fairly in production

For seed companies that need organic supply chain certification rather than a finished product safety claim, GOTS is the relevant standard. For a detailed comparison, see our [GOTS vs OEKO-TEX guide for cotton bags for seeds](/blogs/gots-vs-oeko-tex-cotton-bags-seeds).

## Why Seed Companies and Garden Retailers Require OEKO-TEX

**UK garden retail chains** - including Dobbies, Blue Diamond, Haskins, and RHS-affiliated retailers - require OEKO-TEX Standard 100 documentation on any textile product entering their range. This is not a stated preference; it is a compliance gate. Without a valid certificate number you can show to a buyer at a range review, textile-based seed packaging will not make it onto the shelf.

**US organic farm supply:** OEKO-TEX is recognised in the US by the farm supply market, particularly among buyers who want to show that cotton bags used with edible seed varieties contain no chemical residue that could transfer to the seed or the soil. It is not a regulatory requirement in the US (unlike EU REACH), but it is increasingly requested by buyers for premium organic seed programmes.

**Consumer confidence:** A cotton seed bag carrying the OEKO-TEX Standard 100 label communicates a verifiable safety claim directly to end consumers. For retail seed companies selling premium or children's garden product lines, this label serves a dual function: compliance with retailer requirements and a consumer-facing trust marker.

**Edible seed varieties:** Some seed companies specifically request OEKO-TEX for bags used with herbs, vegetables, or sprout seeds - products where the bag is in direct contact with seed that will ultimately be consumed. OEKO-TEX Class II (direct skin contact) and Class I (products for babies) are the relevant product classes for this use case.

## OEKO-TEX Product Classes: Which One Applies?

OEKO-TEX Standard 100 divides products into four classes based on intended use:

| Class | Description | Relevant for |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Class I | Products for babies and toddlers | Not typical for seed bags |
| **Class II** | **Products with direct skin contact** | **Most retail seed bags - customers handle the bag directly** |
| Class III | Products without direct skin contact | Inner linings, bulk seed bags not handled by consumers |
| Class IV | Decoration materials | Not applicable to seed bags |

Most retail-facing cotton seed bags should be certified to **OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class II**. When requesting a sample or quote from an Indian manufacturer, specify "OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class II certified" rather than just "OEKO-TEX certified" - the class matters for the certification document you receive.

## Verifying a Supplier's OEKO-TEX Certificate

OEKO-TEX certificates are publicly verifiable at [oeko-tex.com/en/apply-here/certificate-database](https://www.oeko-tex.com). Every valid certificate has a unique number. Enter the certificate number on the OEKO-TEX website to confirm:

- The certificate is current (not expired)
- The company named on the certificate matches your supplier
- The certified product categories include cotton bags or comparable textile bags
- The product class is Class II (or higher)

Do not accept a certificate that does not match on any of these checks. A certificate from a different company, or one that only covers yarn or fabric (not finished bags), does not make your finished bags OEKO-TEX compliant.

Also verify that the certificate covers the production facility where your bags will actually be made. Some Indian trading companies present certificates from a facility they buy from, while actually sourcing from a different manufacturer. A factory-direct supplier's certificate names their own facility.

## OEKO-TEX Cotton Seed Bag Specifications from India

When ordering OEKO-TEX certified cotton seed bags from India, confirm the following in your purchase order:

**Fabric weight (GSM):** The most common range for seed bags is 100 to 200 GSM. 100 to 120 GSM (muslin weight) suits small seed varieties (herbs, flowers, fine vegetables). 150 to 200 GSM (twill or light canvas) suits larger seeds and provides a better surface for screen printing.

**Drawstring type:** Specify natural cotton cord, not polyester or nylon. OEKO-TEX certification covers the bag construction as a whole - a certified bag body fitted with a non-certified polyester drawstring creates a compliance gap. Confirm the cord material is included under the certification.

**Stitching thread:** Specify natural cotton thread. Same rationale - polyester thread on a certified cotton bag body is inconsistent with a whole-product safety claim.

**Print inks:** If you want printed bags, specify water-based inks. Plastisol (PVC-based) inks are inconsistent with OEKO-TEX compliance for most applications and are generally unnecessary for fabric printing.

**Bag dimensions:** Standard wholesale sizes from India:

| Size | Dimensions | Best for |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Small | 8 x 12 cm to 12 x 16 cm | Herb seeds, wildflower seeds |
| Medium | 14 x 20 cm to 18 x 24 cm | Vegetable seeds, annual flowers |
| Large | 22 x 32 cm | Beans, peas, sunflower, squash |
| Custom | Any dimension from 6 x 10 cm upwards | Custom SKUs |

## MOQ and Lead Times for OEKO-TEX Cotton Seed Bags

Wholesale minimum order quantities from India:

- Unprinted OEKO-TEX bags: 1,000 to 2,000 units per size
- Screen-printed OEKO-TEX bags (1 to 2 colours): 1,000 to 2,000 units per design
- Screen-printed OEKO-TEX bags (3 to 4 colours): 2,000 units per design

Production lead time is 25 to 35 working days from sample approval. Sea freight to UK ports (Felixstowe, Southampton): 18 to 25 days. Sea freight to US East Coast ports: 25 to 30 days.

**Price premium:** OEKO-TEX certified cotton fabric from India carries a 15 to 25% price premium over non-certified standard fabric at equivalent GSM. For retail seed brands where the OEKO-TEX label supports a premium shelf price, this premium is typically absorbed easily in margin. For commodity seed distribution, non-certified fabric at lower cost is also available.
