## What Is Muslin?

Muslin is a plain-weave, unfinished cotton fabric. The defining characteristics are:

- **Plain weave:** warp and weft threads cross at right angles in a simple over-under pattern, with no twill or satin variation
- **Unfinished surface:** muslin is not calendered (pressed smooth), which gives it its characteristic soft, slightly textured hand
- **Low GSM:** muslin ranges from about 60 GSM (very sheer, like cheesecloth) to about 120 GSM (medium-weight, used for bags and food packaging)
- **Natural colour:** unbleached muslin is an off-white or cream colour from the natural cotton fibre; bleached muslin is white

The term "muslin" covers a range of weights and weave densities. When ordering wholesale, always specify the GSM (grams per square metre) and whether you want unbleached (natural), bleached (white), or a specific dyed colour.

## Muslin Bag Applications and What to Specify for Each

### Herb, Spice, and Tea Bags

**Use:** loose leaf tea brewing bags (reusable), spice satchels for stocks and broths, herb drying bags

**Specification:**
- GSM: 60 to 80 GSM (fine enough for infusion while retaining loose leaf particles)
- Bleached white for food applications (OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified fabric recommended)
- Drawstring or open-top hem closure
- Size: typically 8cm x 10cm to 15cm x 20cm depending on portion size

**Key standard:** For food contact applications, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification verifies the fabric contains no harmful substances at the contact level. This certification is commercially important for food-grade wholesale buyers in the US and EU.

### Retail Gift Bags and Jewellery Pouches

**Use:** retail packaging for jewellery, crystals, candles, skincare, artisan products

**Specification:**
- GSM: 90 to 110 GSM (enough body to hold shape and print cleanly)
- Bleached white or a specific dyed colour to match brand palette
- Drawstring closure (cotton cord or ribbon)
- Size: from 7cm x 9cm (small jewellery pouch) to 25cm x 35cm (gift bag)
- Printing: screen print or flexographic print with brand logo, up to 3 colours

### Seed Storage and Agricultural Bags

**Use:** seed storage, organic farming supply chains, seed company branding

**Specification:**
- GSM: 100 to 120 GSM (heavier than tea bag fabric to handle seed weight and rough handling)
- Unbleached natural or bleached white
- Drawstring or flat sewn-top closure
- Size: 15cm x 20cm to 40cm x 55cm depending on seed volume
- Printing: brand name, variety, lot number

For more on the performance comparison between cotton and plastic in seed storage, see our guide on [cotton bags vs plastic bags for seed storage](/blogs/cotton-bags-vs-plastic-bags-seed-storage).

### Promotional Merchandise and Event Favours

**Use:** branded giveaways, conference bags, wedding favours, corporate gifts

**Specification:**
- GSM: 80 to 100 GSM
- Natural unbleached for a craft aesthetic; white for cleaner print contrast
- Drawstring closure
- Size: 15cm x 20cm (standard favour size) to 30cm x 40cm (usable small tote)
- Printing: up to 4 colours screen print or full-colour sublimation print on white fabric

## How Muslin Bags Are Manufactured in India

Understanding the supply chain helps you ask better questions of potential suppliers.

**Cotton growing:** India's largest cotton-growing states are Gujarat, Maharashtra, Telangana, and Rajasthan. Rajkot (Gujarat) is a major hub for cotton processing and packaging manufacturing.

**Spinning:** Raw cotton is ginned, baled, and spun into yarn. Muslin yarn is typically combed cotton, producing a smoother thread with fewer surface fibres than carded yarn.

**Weaving:** Yarn is woven on power looms into fabric rolls (typically 90cm or 110cm wide). Plain weave is the simplest weave pattern and the fastest to produce.

**Cutting and stitching:** Fabric rolls are cut to blank dimensions, folded, and stitched on industrial sewing machines. Drawstring channels are hemmed and the cord is threaded.

**Printing (if required):** Screen printing is applied before stitching (flat printing on fabric) or after (bag printing). For full-colour or photographic designs, sublimation printing onto white fabric is used.

**Quality check:** Bags are inspected for stitch quality, dimensions, and print registration before packing.

**Export packing:** Bags are folded and packed in cardboard cartons for sea freight export.

This integrated supply chain in India produces muslin bags at a fraction of the cost of equivalent manufacturing in the UK or US, even after sea freight and import duties.

## Minimum Order Quantities and Lead Times

**Minimum order quantities (MOQ):** Most Indian muslin bag manufacturers set an MOQ of 500 to 1,000 bags per size and colour for plain (unprinted) bags. Printed bags typically require 1,000 to 2,000 units per design to amortise screen setup costs.

For wholesale buyers looking to carry multiple sizes or colours, the total order across all SKUs often needs to reach 5,000 units or more to qualify for freight-viable pricing from Indian manufacturers.

**Production lead time:** 15 to 20 working days for plain bags; 20 to 30 working days for printed bags (additional time for screen preparation and drying between print runs).

**Sea freight transit:** 22 to 28 days to US East Coast ports; 18 to 25 days to UK ports.

**Total order-to-delivery:** 8 to 12 weeks for first orders. Establish a reorder schedule once you know your velocity.

## Certifications to Ask For

When sourcing muslin bags from India for retail or food applications, the following certifications add commercial value and reduce compliance risk:

**OEKO-TEX Standard 100:** Tests the finished fabric for over 100 harmful substances. This is the standard most UK and US retailers require for textile products in food contact or children's product categories. Ask for the certificate number and verify it at oeko-tex.com.

**GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard):** Covers the entire supply chain from cotton growing through textile manufacturing. Required if you want to make an organic cotton claim on your product. See our guide on [organic cotton seed bags](/cotton-seed-bags/organic) for more on GOTS certification in agricultural contexts.

**ISO 9001:** Indicates quality management system certification at the manufacturing facility. Not a product standard, but a useful baseline indicator of a well-managed manufacturer.

**FSC or BCI (Better Cotton Initiative):** Covers the sourcing of the raw cotton fibre. BCI-certified cotton comes from farms that have met improved farming practice standards for water use, pesticide use, and worker welfare.

## Pricing Structure: What to Expect

Pricing for muslin bags from India depends on four main variables: GSM, size, quantity, and printing. Without specific numbers, the general structure is:

- Unprinted, plain muslin bags are the lowest-cost option
- Price per unit decreases significantly at 5,000 units vs 1,000 units
- Each print colour adds cost (screen setup plus ink cost)
- Heavier GSM (120 vs 80) costs more due to greater fabric use per bag
- OEKO-TEX or GOTS certified fabric carries a 15 to 25% premium over non-certified fabric

Request a quote with your specifications for accurate pricing. Always compare quotes on an equivalent specification - a 100 GSM OEKO-TEX bag is not comparable in price to a 70 GSM non-certified bag.
