## Who Buys Corrugated Boxes for Apparel

Corrugated packaging is used at multiple points in the fashion supply chain:

**Direct-to-consumer ecommerce brands** - Online fashion retailers shipping individual orders to customers use corrugated mailers and RSC (Regular Slotted Carton) boxes as the primary outgoing package. For brands where the unboxing experience is part of the brand story, custom-printed corrugated with interior messaging and brand colours replaces the plain brown box with a branded moment. Our [custom-printed corrugated boxes guide for ecommerce brands](/blogs/custom-printed-corrugated-boxes-ecommerce-guide) covers print quality, turnaround, and specifications for online retail packaging in detail.

**Wholesale clothing distributors** - Companies supplying garments in bulk to retailers use corrugated outer cartons as transit packaging. At this scale, the priority is dimensional consistency (cartons must stack efficiently in a container and on warehouse racking), board strength for heavy garment loads, and plain or single-colour print sufficient for SKU identification.

**Boutique and independent fashion brands** - Smaller fashion labels sourcing packaging to differentiate their product use corrugated as both the shipping container and a brand statement. A folded garment arriving in a printed box with the brand name and a clean interior message is perceived as a premium experience even before the product is seen.

**Apparel subscription box companies** - Monthly clothing subscription services ship curated garment packages in branded corrugated boxes on a recurring basis. These buyers need reliable volume supply on a rolling schedule. ## Corrugated Box Types for Fashion Packaging

Different stages of the fashion supply chain call for different box formats:

**RSC (Regular Slotted Carton)** - The standard corrugated box format. Four flaps on top and bottom meet in the middle when sealed. Used for shipping folded garments and wholesale transit cartons. The most cost-efficient format for high-volume runs. Available in single-wall (B or C flute for lighter garments) or double-wall (BC flute for heavier items or fragile accessories).

**Mailer boxes (auto-lock bottom)** - A rigid, self-locking format that opens and closes cleanly without tape. Preferred by direct-to-consumer fashion brands for individual order fulfilment. The interior can include printed messaging, logo placement, or a partial-colour interior print. More expensive per unit than RSC, but the construction adds a premium unboxing experience that RSC cannot replicate.

**Flat-pack garment boxes** - Used for shirts, blouses, and folded items where crease-free presentation is important. Shallow dimensions with a lid-and-base construction. Common for premium fashion retail, in-store packaging, and gift packaging.

**Outer shipping cartons with inner fitments** - For mixed-SKU wholesale orders where several items ship together, an outer RSC carton with a custom inner divider or separator keeps garments organised and prevents damage in transit. Indian manufacturers produce both the carton and the fitment from the same corrugated sheet, which simplifies quoting and production scheduling.

## Print Quality for Fashion Brands

![Person sealing a corrugated shipping box for apparel and fashion brand order fulfilment](https://images.pexels.com/photos/4246120/pexels-photo-4246120.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1200)
*Custom-printed corrugated boxes from Indian manufacturers use flexographic or offset-litho printing to carry fashion brand logos and colours accurately.*

Fashion brands have higher print quality expectations than most corrugated buyers. What to know before specifying print:

**Flexographic printing** is the standard process for corrugated in India and globally. It handles logos, solid brand colours, and clean graphic layouts well at high volumes. Colour accuracy for brand-specific Pantone references is achievable with experienced suppliers; always verify with a printed sample before committing to a full production run.

**Offset-litho laminated printing** produces the highest print quality on corrugated - comparable to premium retail packaging. A separate litho-printed sheet is laminated to the corrugated board. This process requires higher minimum quantities (typically 10,000 units per design) but the print quality is distinctly higher than flexo. Relevant for luxury fashion and premium retail boxes.

**Surface coatings** - Gloss laminate, matt laminate, and soft-touch laminate are all available from Indian manufacturers. Soft-touch (velvet laminate) is a premium option increasingly specified by fashion brands for its distinctive tactile quality.

**Colour matching** - State Pantone references in your brief. For brand-critical colours, request printed samples before final approval. What appears correct on screen may not match the Pantone reference on the finished board.

**Interior print** - Printing the inside of a mailer box is available from most Indian suppliers. Brand messaging, a repeating pattern, a solid colour interior, or a thank-you note printed directly into the box is a differentiator at modest cost per unit compared to inserting a separate printed card.

## Specifications for Apparel Packaging

Getting dimensions and board spec right is the most common first-order challenge. Key points:

**Dimension sequence** - Always state internal dimensions as Length x Width x Depth (LxWxD) in millimetres. For folded garments, measure the folded item carefully and add 10 to 15mm in each direction to allow comfortable loading without forcing.

**Board grade by garment weight:**

| Application | Recommended Grade |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Light garments (t-shirts, lingerie) | Single-wall B or E flute, 150-200 gsm liner |
| Medium garments (shirts, trousers) | Single-wall C flute, 175 gsm liner |
| Heavy garments (knitwear, denim) | Single-wall BC or double-wall CC |
| Mixed-SKU wholesale carton | Double-wall BC flute |
| Premium retail or unboxing box | E flute with litho laminate |

**Stacking strength** - For palletised shipping, state how many boxes will be stacked vertically. The manufacturer sizes the board to handle the compressive load. Overpacking (specifying heavier board than the garment weight requires) adds unnecessary freight weight and cost.

**Tolerance** - Standard manufacturing tolerance is +/- 3mm on each dimension. For automated fulfilment operations where boxes must fit precisely in conveyor or machine-feed systems, state the required tolerance explicitly.

**Inserts and void fill** - If tissue paper, branded card inserts, or protective fitments need to fit inside the box, specify these dimensions alongside the outer carton. Indian manufacturers can produce corrugated fitments and source complementary inserts through the same supply relationship.

## Sustainable Packaging for Fashion Brands

![Fashion brand buyer reviewing sustainable apparel packaging options sourced from India](https://images.pexels.com/photos/7679852/pexels-photo-7679852.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1200)
*Sustainable packaging is a growing requirement for fashion brands responding to customer expectations and retailer sustainability programmes.*

The fashion industry faces sustained pressure on packaging sustainability, and corrugated packaging is a visible, manageable part of the supply chain response. What Indian corrugated manufacturers can offer:

**FSC-certified board** - Forest Stewardship Council certification confirms the virgin fibre content comes from responsibly managed forests. Available from established Indian manufacturers, and relevant for brands making verifiable sustainability claims about their packaging.

**Recycled content boards** - Corrugated boxes made with a high percentage of recycled fibre (typically 70 to 100% recycled liner) are available at comparable pricing to virgin board. Recycled content is standard for plain brown corrugated; for high-print-quality applications, a mix of recycled core and virgin liner is the standard option.

**Right-sizing** - The single most effective sustainable packaging change a fashion brand can make is eliminating excess void space in their shipping box. A box sized precisely to the garment reduces dunnage, pack weight, and the number of boxes per shipping container. Indian manufacturers produce custom sizes as standard - there is no surcharge for a non-standard dimension.

**Biodegradable coatings** - Standard soft-touch laminates use plastic film, which can complicate recyclability in markets with strict packaging regulations. Water-based and aqueous laminates are available as an alternative for brands with strict packaging recyclability requirements.

India's corrugated industry has invested significantly in FSC certification over the past decade, driven by UK and European buyer requirements. ## Why India for Apparel Packaging

India is already a major apparel manufacturing hub for US and UK fashion brands. The same supply chain infrastructure - established freight lanes, export documentation experience, Gujarat port access - that makes India competitive for garment manufacturing also makes it competitive for the corrugated packaging that ships those garments.

**Cost advantage** - Indian corrugated manufacturers operate at significantly lower overhead than US or UK equivalents. The combination of lower energy costs, raw material proximity, and competitive labour produces unit prices that allow fashion brands to invest more in print quality and box specification for the same packaging budget.

**Custom print at scale** - India's corrugated export manufacturers have invested in flexographic and offset printing equipment that meets international quality standards. This was driven by years of supplying US and UK brands with print requirements that domestic trade printers met only at prohibitive cost.

**Consolidated shipments** - Fashion brands sourcing corrugated boxes alongside tissue paper, ribbon, branded inserts, or other packaging components from India can consolidate multiple items in a single shipping container. This simplifies logistics and reduces per-unit freight cost across the packaging range.

**Export documentation familiarity** - Indian packaging exporters are experienced with the full documentation chain US and UK brands require: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and FSC chain-of-custody documentation where specified.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the minimum order quantity for corrugated boxes from India?**
Most Indian manufacturers start at 5,000 units per specification (size and print combination). At this quantity, a production run is economically viable and per-unit pricing is competitive with what a domestic trade printer charges at the same volume. Orders of 50,000 units and above qualify for the strongest unit pricing and can support a dedicated production run with preferential scheduling.

**Can I order different sizes and prints in the same order?**
Yes, but each size and print combination has its own minimum quantity. A single order can include several SKUs - for example, three garment sizes requiring three box dimensions - provided each meets the per-specification minimum. Discuss your SKU range upfront so the manufacturer can advise on how to consolidate efficiently.

**How do I verify print quality before committing to a full run?**
Request a printed pre-production sample, not just a digital proof. A physical sample shows exactly how your brand colours reproduce on the specific corrugated board and liner being used. Colour variation between a digital proof and the finished box is the most common first-order complaint; a physical sample eliminates this risk before production begins.

**Will garments arrive undamaged through sea freight from India?**
Yes, provided the box specification is correct for the weight and fragility of the garments being shipped. Single-wall corrugated handles folded apparel well under standard sea freight conditions. For luxury items, accessories, or items that require crease-free delivery, flat-pack box construction combined with tissue paper or inner fitments provides additional protection. The sea voyage in a sealed container introduces no unusual environmental risk to garments.

**What does FSC certification cover for corrugated boxes?**
FSC certification confirms the fibre content of the corrugated board comes from forests managed to FSC standards. It does not cover printing inks, laminates, or other components. For brands making broad packaging sustainability claims, the full bill of materials - board, inks, coatings, tape - should be reviewed against those claims separately.

**Can I include branded inserts or tissue paper in the same shipment?**
Yes. A printed thank-you card, tissue paper with a pattern, or brand stickers can all be specified as part of the same shipment from India. We can source complementary packaging components from within the Gujarat supply cluster and consolidate in a single container, reducing freight cost and simplifying the import process.
