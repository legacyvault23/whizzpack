## What Corrugated Packaging Pet Food Brands Actually Need

The UK pet food market exceeded £3.5 billion in 2024. The US market is over $50 billion. Both are growing. And behind every bag of dry kibble, every tray of wet food, and every pouch of treats is a supply chain that depends on corrugated secondary packaging.

For brands importing corrugated boxes from India, the key decisions are: what board grade to specify, whether to use food-safe liner, how to set up shelf-ready vs. shipping outers, and what certifications matter in UK and US retail.

This guide covers each of those decisions.

## Types of Corrugated Boxes Used in Pet Food Packaging

**Shipping outers** are the standard RSC (regular slotted container) box used to ship multiple units. These do not need to be attractive but must survive palletised sea freight from India to the UK or US. For most dry kibble and treat formats, 3-ply (single wall) B flute is the minimum. For wet food (heavier cans and pouches), 5-ply (double wall) BC flute is the standard.

**Shelf-ready packaging (SRP)** - also called retail-ready packaging or RRP - is designed so that retailers can cut a perforation and place the tray directly onto the shelf. Branded, printed, and with clear product visibility. B flute (3mm) or E flute (1.5mm, for a sharper print) are both common. See our detailed breakdown of [B flute vs C flute vs E flute options](/blogs/b-flute-vs-c-flute-vs-e-flute-corrugated-boxes) for guidance on choosing between them.

**Display cases** are shallow, open-top boxes that hold 4-12 retail units for end-of-aisle display. Common in the treats and chew category.

**E-commerce mailer boxes** are increasingly used by DTC pet food brands. Typically 3-ply E flute, fitted, with the brand printed on the outside.

The most common specifications for pet food corrugated packaging:

## Food Safety and Liner Requirements

This is the most commonly misunderstood area for brands new to corrugated.

**Virgin kraft liner** is required for any corrugated box that comes into direct contact with food product, or where there is a risk of product contact through packaging perforations, tears or handling. Virgin kraft contains no recycled content and therefore carries no risk of mineral oil aromatic hydrocarbons (MOAH) contamination from recycled newspaper ink.

For indirect contact (the outer shipping box for a sealed bag of kibble), recycled board is acceptable in most UK and US markets. EU PPWR and US FDA 21 CFR both draw this distinction clearly.

If your packaging agency or retail buyer specifies "food-contact grade" board, they mean virgin kraft liner. This should be called out explicitly in your RFQ to avoid receiving recycled board on the first sample.

## Certifications UK and US Buyers Request

**FSC (Forest Stewardship Council):** Most major UK and US pet food retailers - including Pets at Home, PetSmart, and Chewy - require FSC-certified packaging or are actively moving toward it. FSC certifies the paper fibre in the board, not the design. Whizzpack holds FSC chain-of-custody certification, meaning our corrugated boxes can carry the FSC Mix or FSC 100% label.

**ISO 22000 / FSSC 22000:** These food safety management certifications apply primarily to the board mill, not the box converter. If your QA team requests FSSC 22000 from the corrugated box supplier, clarify whether they mean the mill (which supplies board) or the converter (which cuts and prints boxes). Most Indian box manufacturers are ISO 9001 certified.

**GMP:** Good Manufacturing Practice compliance for the box plant applies mainly to pharmaceutical packaging. For pet food secondary packaging, GMP is not a standard requirement, though some premium natural pet food brands apply similar documentation.

## Custom Printing for Shelf-Ready and Branded Packaging

Shelf-ready packaging requires print quality good enough to represent the brand on-shelf. The main options from Indian manufacturers:

**Flexographic printing:** 1-4 colour, lower plate setup cost, minimum order from 5,000 units, suitable for spot-colour brand logos and product information. Most shelf-ready pet food packaging from India uses 2-3 colour flexo.

**Digital printing:** No plate cost, full colour, ideal for initial runs or where multiple SKUs differ only in flavour or variety. Digital minimum runs can be as low as 1,000 units, making it practical for product launches or limited editions.

For shipping outers, most brands specify black-on-kraft with the brand name and barcode only. Full print is not needed on a box that goes into a warehouse and never reaches the consumer.

## Sustainability Requirements from UK and US Retailers

Sustainability pressure in pet food packaging has increased sharply since 2022. The key requirements from UK and US retail buyers:

**FSC certification:** As above, this is now standard across most major UK chains and a growing requirement at US retailers.

**Recyclability:** Corrugated board is inherently recyclable. However, laminated or gloss-varnished finishes can affect recyclability certification. Uncoated kraft with water-based flexo print is the safest specification if your buyer requires a "fully recyclable" on-pack claim.

**Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR):** UK brands must register under the UK Packaging Regulations (reformed 2023) and report packaging placed on market. Indian-made corrugated boxes count as packaging placed on market by the UK importer. This is an administrative compliance step, not a constraint on the box specification itself.

Corrugated packaging from India is among the most sustainable secondary packaging options available, provided the board carries FSC certification and the print finish is kept to water-based ink or varnish.

## Treat and Snack Brands: Specific Packaging Considerations

![Person packing dog treats into kraft paper bags at a workbench](https://images.pexels.com/photos/7310128/pexels-photo-7310128.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1200)

Treat and snack brands have slightly different needs from main meal kibble brands:

- **Smaller units, higher SKU count:** A treat brand might carry 10-15 flavour or size variants. E flute corrugated gives a sharper print surface for differentiation at point of sale.
- **Display-ready formats:** Treats are heavily impulse-bought. Display cases and counter-top shelf-ready trays are more common than plain shipping outers.
- **Mixed origin retail:** UK pet specialty chains and grocery multiples often carry treat brands alongside private label. Print registration and colour consistency across production runs matters more here than in commodity kibble.

## FAQ

**What board grade do pet food brands typically use for shipping outers?**

For bags of dry kibble up to 5kg, 3-ply single wall B flute is the standard. For heavier bags (10-15kg) or wet food in cans and pouches, 5-ply double wall BC flute is recommended for its higher compression strength and burst resistance.

**Do corrugated boxes for pet food need to be food-grade?**

For outer shipping boxes where food is not in direct contact, recycled board is generally acceptable. For any box that may contact product directly (shelf-ready trays, display cases), virgin kraft liner is the correct specification. Always confirm with your regulatory team or retail buyer before ordering.

**Is FSC certification required for pet food packaging in the UK?**

FSC is not legally mandated but is a commercial requirement for most UK major retailers including Pets at Home, Tesco, Sainsbury's, and Asda. Brands without FSC-certified packaging may be prevented from listing new SKUs.
