US brands that switch packaging supply from domestic or Chinese sources to India typically save between 35% and 50% on per-unit corrugated costs at comparable quality. The savings are real - but the process is different from domestic procurement, and first-time importers often underestimate lead times, overestimate their ability to manage quality remotely, or miss a documentation requirement that delays customs clearance.

This guide walks through every step of importing corrugated boxes from India to the US, from placing your first inquiry to receiving boxes at your warehouse or Amazon FBA facility.

## Step 1 - Decide Your Specification Before Contacting Suppliers

The most common mistake first-time importers make is sending a vague inquiry: "I need corrugated boxes, 12x10x8 inches, 10,000 units." An Indian manufacturer needs considerably more information to produce an accurate quote, and vague inquiries result in quotes that are either inflated (the manufacturer builds in a margin for uncertainty) or inaccurate (the quote doesn't reflect what you actually need).

Before contacting any manufacturer, prepare the following:

**Internal dimensions:** Always specify internal dimensions (the space your product needs to fit inside), not external. The manufacturer calculates the blank and board dimensions from your internal spec.

**Board specification:** At minimum, state the flute type (B, C, E, or BC double wall) and the ply count (3-ply single wall or 5-ply double wall). If you know your ECT rating requirement (32 lb/in for most FBA applications, 44+ for heavier products), include it. See our [B flute vs C flute vs E flute guide](/blogs/b-flute-vs-c-flute-vs-e-flute-corrugated-boxes) if you are not sure which flute your application needs.

**Construction:** RSC (Regular Slotted Container) is the standard for most US shipping applications and Amazon FBA. State "RSC construction" unless you need a die-cut or custom format.

**Print requirement:** Plain kraft, one-colour logo, two-colour, or full-colour four-colour print. If printing, specify whether the board will be pre-printed or if you want lithographic laminate.

**Certifications:** FSC chain-of-custody if required by your retailer. ISTA 2A test results if required for FBA. ECT and burst strength test reports are standard.

**Annual volume and order frequency:** Manufacturers price more competitively for buyers with predictable repeat volumes. Even an estimate helps.

## Step 2 - Understand US Import Duty and HTS Classification

Corrugated boxes from India import into the US under HTS heading 4819. The most common subheadings are:

- **4819.20.0020** - Folding cartons of corrugated paper or paperboard (most standard RSC boxes)
- **4819.20.0040** - Other cartons and cases of corrugated paper or paperboard

The US MFN (Most Favoured Nation) duty rate under 4819.20 is **0%** for India. No Section 301 tariffs apply to India - these are specific to China. This means corrugated boxes from India clear US customs duty-free under standard MFN.

Additional charges at customs that US importers should budget for:

- **Merchandise Processing Fee (MPF):** 0.3464% of customs value, minimum $32.71 per entry
- **Harbor Maintenance Fee (HMF):** 0.125% of customs value for ocean freight entries
- **ISF (Importer Security Filing):** A flat fee charged by your customs broker, typically $25-50 per shipment

For a full breakdown of HTS codes, duty rates, and what documentation to expect from an Indian supplier, see our [HTS code 4819 guide for US importers](/blogs/corrugated-box-import-duty-us-hts-code).

## Step 3 - Request Samples Before Production

Never commit to a production order from a new Indian manufacturer without first reviewing physical samples. This is non-negotiable, and any reputable manufacturer will provide samples to serious buyers.

**What to test when you receive samples:**

Box dimensions: Measure the internal dimensions against your spec. Allow up to 2mm tolerance either way.

Board stiffness: Compress the box from the top with your palm. For C flute 3-ply, the board should not collapse at hand pressure. For BC double wall 5-ply, it should feel substantially rigid.

Burst and ECT test reports: Request these with the sample. The test report should state the GSM of the liner and medium, the ECT value, and the burst factor. If the manufacturer cannot provide test reports, that is a warning sign.

Print quality: If ordering printed boxes, request a sample with your artwork applied. Check colour accuracy against your Pantone reference, clean edge definition, and consistency across the sample set.

Flap alignment: The four top flaps and four bottom flaps of an RSC box should meet cleanly at the centre seam without gaps or overlap. Misalignment indicates die tooling issues.

Corner crush resistance: Apply thumb pressure to the bottom corner of the assembled box. The corner should hold its shape without buckling.

## Step 4 - Production Lead Times From India

Production lead time from an Indian corrugated manufacturer after artwork approval and order confirmation is typically **18-28 working days** for a standard RSC order. Factors that extend this:

- First-ever run with a new die (die tooling takes 3-5 working days)
- Custom print in four colours (platemaking takes 5-7 working days)
- FSC-certified board specification (requires certified board to be sourced - add 3-5 days)
- Large orders requiring multiple press runs (add 5-10 days depending on volume)

**Production schedule planning:** For a first order, build in 10-15% extra time beyond the manufacturer's stated lead time. Indian public holidays (Diwali, Republic Day, Independence Day, Holi) can pause production for 1-5 working days depending on when your order falls.

## Step 5 - Sea Freight Transit to US Ports

Ocean freight from India to US ports takes:

- **US East Coast (New York, Baltimore, Savannah, Charleston):** 22-28 days from Mundra or JNPT (Nhava Sheva, Mumbai)
- **US West Coast (Los Angeles, Long Beach, Seattle):** 25-32 days from Mundra

Add 5-7 working days for customs clearance and inland delivery after the vessel arrives at port.

**Total timeline from order confirmation to US warehouse:**

| Stage | Days |
|---|---|
| Production (standard RSC, first print run) | 20-28 days |
| Container stuffing and inland freight to port | 3-5 days |
| Sea freight to US East Coast | 22-28 days |
| Customs clearance and delivery | 5-7 days |
| **Total** | **50-68 days** |

For repeat orders with an established specification, production compresses to 15-20 days and total lead time is typically 45-55 days.

**Practical implication:** US brands should reorder corrugated boxes when they have 65-75 days of stock remaining. Many FBA sellers and DTC brands set a reorder trigger at 2.5 months of sales cover.

## Step 6 - Container and Freight Planning

A standard 40ft container holds approximately 20,000-30,000 RSC boxes, depending on box dimensions and whether boxes are nested (flat-packed inside each other, which significantly increases box count per container).

If your order is below full container load (FCL), you will ship LCL (Less than Container Load). LCL consolidates your cargo with other importers' shipments in a shared container. LCL transit times are typically 5-10 days longer than FCL, and LCL per-unit freight costs are higher. At order volumes above approximately 2,000-3,000 large boxes, FCL often becomes more economical.

**Incoterms:** Most Indian manufacturers quote FOB (Free on Board). This means the manufacturer loads your goods onto the vessel at the Indian port, and you are responsible for freight, insurance, and delivery from that point. EXW (Ex Works) puts more logistics responsibility on you; CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight) means the manufacturer arranges and pays for freight to the destination port.

For US first-time importers, FOB is the most common and simplest arrangement. Engage a US-side freight forwarder to arrange the ocean freight leg.

## Step 7 - Customs Clearance

For customs clearance of corrugated boxes from India, you will need a licensed customs broker (CBP requires a broker for commercial imports above $2,500). Your broker will file the entry and handle CBP examination if selected.

**Documents your Indian manufacturer provides:**

- Commercial invoice (seller and buyer details, description, quantity, unit price, total value, country of origin)
- Packing list (carton count, gross weight, net weight, dimensions per pallet)
- Bill of lading or sea waybill
- Certificate of origin (if required by your broker for preference claims)
- Test certificates (ECT, burst strength) on request

**Documents you or your broker arrange:**

- ISF (Importer Security Filing) - must be submitted 24 hours before vessel loading at origin port
- CBP entry form (3461 or 7501 depending on entry type)
- FDA prior notice is not required for corrugated packaging (it is not a food contact material in this context)

## Amazon FBA-Specific Considerations

US Amazon FBA sellers importing corrugated boxes from India should note:

**Box size limits:** Maximum 25 inches on any single side, 50 lb (22.7 kg) maximum per carton for standard items.

**ECT rating:** Amazon recommends minimum 32 lb/in ECT for FBA shipment boxes. Specify this explicitly in your purchase order.

**Suffocation warning labels:** Products in the Baby category (and certain categories with poly bags) require suffocation warning labels on each unit. The Indian manufacturer prints these in the artwork. Confirm label size and placement meet Amazon's category requirements.

**FNSKU label area:** Plan an unprinted panel on the box exterior for FNSKU label placement. Amazon requires the label to be on the exterior of each unit.

## Common First-Order Mistakes to Avoid

**Ordering without a sample review.** The cost of a sample set from India is typically absorbed by the manufacturer for serious buyers. Skip this step and you have no recourse if production quality is off.

**Not specifying internal dimensions.** Your product needs to fit inside. Always specify internal, never external.

**Ignoring monsoon season humidity.** June through September is India's monsoon. Corrugated board absorbs moisture and loses strength in humid conditions. For orders shipped during monsoon, specify moisture-resistant liner grades or request shrink-wrapped pallets.

**Underestimating total lead time.** Many first-time importers budget 4 weeks from order to delivery. The correct budget is 10-14 weeks for a first order.

**Not verifying test certificates.** Request ECT and burst strength test reports for every production batch. Reputable manufacturers supply these routinely.
