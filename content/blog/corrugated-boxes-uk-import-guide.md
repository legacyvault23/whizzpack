## Why UK Importers Are Choosing Indian Manufacturers

The economics of importing corrugated boxes from India to the UK have improved significantly over the past few years. Three factors are driving this:

**Direct vessel services to UK ports.** Container shipping routes between Mundra Port (INMUN) in Gujarat and major UK ports - Felixstowe, Southampton, and London Gateway - now operate on relatively short transit times. Transit from Mundra to Felixstowe runs approximately 18 to 22 days, making the planning cycle manageable for buyers with good inventory visibility.

**Post-Brexit UK customs access.** India and the UK are in ongoing trade negotiations, and corrugated paper boxes (HS code 4819.10) currently attract 0 percent import duty into the UK under standard MFN rates. No anti-dumping duties or special tariffs apply to corrugated packaging from India at the time of writing. This creates a clean cost structure that buyers can model accurately.

**UK Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR).** The UK EPR scheme for packaging - which requires importers and producers to pay for the end-of-life cost of their packaging - does not penalise imported packaging differently from domestic packaging. What it does reward is recyclable, high-recycled-content packaging. Indian manufacturers who can supply FSC-certified boards and provide recycled content documentation put UK buyers in a stronger EPR compliance position.

## UK Port Logistics: What to Know

Three UK ports handle most corrugated box container traffic from India:

**Felixstowe (GBFXT)** is the UK's busiest container port and the default destination for most shipments from Mundra and JNPT. Direct vessel services from Mundra to Felixstowe run on weekly schedules with transit times of 18 to 22 days. From Felixstowe, road haulage connects efficiently to the Midlands, London, and the North.

**Southampton (GBSOU)** is an alternative for buyers in the South of England or those needing competitive berth availability. Transit times from Mundra are similar to Felixstowe.

**London Gateway (GBLGP)** is the DP World terminal east of London. It connects well for buyers distributing into Greater London and the Home Counties, and its Inland Rail Terminal links to major distribution hubs.

For most UK buyers, Felixstowe is the default unless there is a specific geographic or forwarder reason to use an alternative.

**Incoterms for first orders:** FOB (Free on Board) at Mundra is the most common starting point. Your UK freight forwarder handles the ocean freight booking, UK customs clearance, and inland delivery. A UK customs broker familiar with paper and board imports will handle the entry efficiently.

## What to Specify When Ordering

Corrugated boxes have a set of technical parameters that must be confirmed before production begins. These are the variables UK buyers need to understand:

### Flute Type

The most common flute types for UK import orders are B flute (3.5mm, good stacking strength), C flute (4mm, balanced performance, most common for general shipping), E flute (1.5mm, smooth surface, good for custom print), and BC double wall (7mm, heavy-duty). If you are unsure which flute type matches your application, see our detailed [flute comparison guide](/blogs/b-flute-vs-c-flute-vs-e-flute-corrugated-boxes).

### Board Grade and GSM

UK buyers often refer to board grades by ECT (Edge Crush Test) rating or bursting strength (Mullen Test). Indian manufacturers use both systems. A standard export shipping box for moderate-weight goods typically specifies 150-175 GSM kraft liner with 120 GSM fluting medium. Heavier goods require higher GSM combinations or double-wall construction.

### FSC Certification

For UK buyers operating under EPR obligations or supplying to retailers with sustainability requirements, FSC chain-of-custody documentation is increasingly expected. Indian manufacturers, including Whizzpack, can supply FSC-certified corrugated board on request. The FSC certificate number is verifiable online. Specify FSC at the inquiry stage - it affects raw material sourcing and may add 5-8 working days to the first order timeline.

### Custom Printing

If you need branded boxes, water-based flexo printing is the standard option. 1 to 4 Pantone colours are achievable on most corrugated boards. Artwork should be supplied in AI or print-ready PDF format. A digital proof is provided before plate production begins. Physical press samples are available for new designs before full-run commitment.

## UK Packaging Waste Regulations and EPR

The UK Packaging Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) scheme requires businesses that place packaging on the UK market to register as a packaging producer if they meet certain thresholds. The scheme calculates an obligation fee based on the weight and type of packaging material placed on the market.

Corrugated cardboard is classified as a recyclable material with an established end-of-life recovery infrastructure. Boxes sourced from India are treated the same as domestically produced boxes under EPR - what matters is the material and its recyclability, not where it was manufactured.

To support EPR reporting, ask your Indian supplier for:
- Recycled content percentage in the board specification
- Material composition data sheet (liner GSM, fluting GSM, virgin vs recycled fibre)
- FSC or PEFC chain-of-custody documentation (where applicable)

A manufacturer who has supplied UK buyers before will have these documents prepared. They are standard export documentation for compliance-focused markets.

## Lead Times and Planning

A typical first order from India to the UK works on this timeline:

- Sample review and specification sign-off: 5-10 working days
- Sample production and shipping to UK: 10-15 working days by air freight
- Production of full order: 18-25 working days from sample approval
- Sea freight Mundra to Felixstowe: 18-22 days
- UK customs clearance and inland delivery: 2-5 working days

Total from first contact to goods in your warehouse: approximately 10-14 weeks for a first order. Repeat orders, where specification is fixed and the supplier relationship established, typically run 8-10 weeks from order to delivery.

Buffer stock planning is important. Maintain enough inventory to cover 12-14 weeks of consumption while your first order is in transit and production.

## Questions to Ask a Manufacturer Before Ordering

Before committing to any Indian corrugated box supplier, run through these questions:

1. Can you supply FSC-certified board, and do you have a current FSC chain-of-custody certificate?
2. What is the recycled content percentage in your standard board specification?
3. Do you have experience exporting to UK buyers? Can you provide a reference?
4. What documentation do you provide with each shipment (test reports, GSM certificates, packing list, commercial invoice)?
5. What is your process for resolving specification deviations discovered after delivery?
6. Can you provide a physical sample before committing to full production?

A manufacturer with genuine UK export experience will answer these directly and quickly.
