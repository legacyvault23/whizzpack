UK buyers sourcing cotton seed bags have three realistic supply options: domestic manufacturers, European suppliers, or Indian factories. Indian manufacturers offer the widest range of specifications, the strongest access to certified organic cotton, and direct factory pricing. The trade-off is a longer supply chain that requires forward planning.

This guide is written for UK buyers specifically: UK port logistics, UK customs duty, relevant UK and EU-origin certifications, and what to ask when evaluating an Indian cotton seed bag manufacturer.

If you are still evaluating cotton bags as a category, our [cotton seed bags sourcing guide for US and UK buyers](/blogs/cotton-seed-bags-sourcing-guide-usa-uk) covers the fundamentals.

## Why UK Buyers Source from India

Cotton seed bag manufacturing is concentrated in India - specifically in Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu, which together account for a large proportion of global cotton bag production. The reasons for sourcing from India rather than domestically or from Europe are practical:

**Certified organic cotton availability.** India grows and gins a significant proportion of the world's organic cotton under GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard) and OCS (Organic Content Standard) certification. UK buyers who need to make organic certification claims on their packaging have far more options with Indian suppliers than with European ones.

**OEKO-TEX compliance.** OEKO-TEX Standard 100 tests finished textile products for harmful substances - pesticide residues, heavy metals, formaldehyde, and other compounds. Many Indian manufacturers of cotton bags carry OEKO-TEX certification, which is directly relevant to UK buyers supplying food-adjacent applications such as seed packaging, dried goods, or children's products.

**MOQ flexibility and customisation.** Indian manufacturers can produce custom sizes, weights, closures, and prints from relatively low minimum order quantities - typically 500 to 1,000 units per specification. This is more flexible than comparable European suppliers at equivalent quality levels.

**Post-Brexit UK customs.** Cotton seed bags and plain cotton bags fall under HS codes 6305.20 (of cotton, for packing goods) or 6217.90 depending on specification. The UK import duty rate is 12 percent MFN for woven cotton bags of this type, which is worth factoring into the landed cost calculation. Buyers importing under significant volumes should check with their customs broker whether any UK Generalised Scheme of Preferences (GSP) rate applies.

## What to Specify

Cotton seed bags have fewer technical parameters than corrugated boxes, but getting the specifications right matters for both performance and certification purposes.

**Fabric weight (GSM).** Cotton bags for seed storage typically run from 100 GSM (lightweight, for small seed packets and retail display) to 200-280 GSM (heavy-duty, for 5kg-25kg bulk seed sacks). The right GSM depends on your load weight, closure type, and whether the bag will be reused.

**Weave type.** Standard plain-weave muslin is the most common for lightweight seed bags. Canvas weave (tighter, heavier) is used for bulk bags. Drill weave sits between the two in weight and tightness. If the bag will be printed, specify a tight weave with a smooth surface - this takes screen and digital print better.

**Closure type.** Drawstring closures (cotton cord threaded through a casing at the top) are the most common for retail seed bags. Tie-top closures (two fabric ties sewn at the opening) are simpler and cheaper. Sewn-shut closures are used for single-use bulk sacks. If your bags will be resealable for customer use, specify drawstring.

**Organic certification.** If you need GOTS-certified bags, specify at the inquiry stage. GOTS certification covers the entire production chain - cotton farming, ginning, spinning, weaving, and sewing - and each stage requires certified processors. Not all Indian manufacturers have full GOTS chain-of-custody. Ask to see the current GOTS certificate and verify the certificate number on the GOTS public database (global-standard.org). For OCS (Organic Content Standard), the certification covers only the fibre content, not processing inputs - it is a weaker claim but still verifiable.

**OEKO-TEX Standard 100.** This certification applies to the finished product and confirms no harmful substances above set limits. If you are supplying food-adjacent applications or products for children, OEKO-TEX is worth requiring.

**Custom print.** Screen printing is standard for cotton bags. 1 to 3 colour designs print cleanly on plain-weave cotton. Full colour options using heat transfer or digital printing are available but less durable on natural cotton. Specify washability requirements if the bags will be reused.

## UK Port Logistics

Sea freight from Indian cotton bag manufacturers in Gujarat typically loads at Mundra Port (INMUN) or Nhava Sheva (INJNP, also called JNPT, in Mumbai). For manufacturers in Tamil Nadu, Chennai Port (INMAA) is more common.

**Felixstowe (GBFXT)** handles the largest volume of UK container imports and is the default for Gujarat-origin shipments from Mundra. Transit time from Mundra to Felixstowe runs approximately 18 to 22 days. From Felixstowe, road haulage reaches most UK distribution points within 1-3 days.

**Southampton (GBSOU)** is an alternative for South England buyers. London Gateway (GBLGP) works well for buyers distributing into Greater London.

Cotton bags are light relative to their volume, so most shipments fill a container on volume before weight. A 40-foot container can hold a large volume of flat-packed cotton bags. Work with your freight forwarder on packing configuration to optimise container fill.

**UK customs:** Budget for 12 percent import duty on woven cotton bags (HS 6305.20) when calculating landed cost. Your customs broker will confirm the exact HS classification and applicable rate for your specific product. VAT at 20 percent is payable on import but recoverable if you are VAT-registered.

## Lead Times for UK Buyers

A typical first order timeline from inquiry to UK warehouse:

- Specification discussion and sample request: 5-7 working days
- Sample production: 5-10 working days (longer for GOTS-certified samples)
- Air freight of sample to UK: 5-7 working days
- Sample review and specification sign-off: your internal time
- Production of full order: 20-30 working days
- Sea freight Mundra to Felixstowe: 18-22 days
- UK customs clearance and inland delivery: 2-5 working days

Allow 12-14 weeks for a first order. Eco-certified materials (GOTS, OCS) may add 5-8 days to production, as the manufacturer may need to pre-order certified inputs.

Repeat orders, once specification is fixed, typically run 8-10 weeks from purchase order to delivery.

## Questions to Ask Before Ordering

1. Do you hold a current GOTS or OCS certificate? Can you share the certificate number for verification?
2. Is your OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification current, and which product categories does it cover?
3. What fabric weights and weave types do you manufacture in-house?
4. Can you produce a physical sample before the full production run?
5. What is your minimum order quantity per specification?
6. What documentation comes with each shipment (material data sheet, packing list, certificate of origin)?
7. Have you exported cotton bags to UK buyers before?
