## Why Source Custom Corrugated Boxes from India?

India is one of the world's largest manufacturers of corrugated packaging. The country's corrugated board industry produces over 3 million metric tonnes per year, with a significant share destined for export markets. Here is what makes India a preferred sourcing destination:

**Strong manufacturing infrastructure:** India has hundreds of FSC-certified, ISO 9001-compliant corrugated box manufacturers with modern printing presses capable of flexographic, offset lithographic, and digital printing.

**Eco-friendly raw materials:** Many Indian manufacturers use recycled kraft paper made from post-consumer waste. Leading suppliers also source board certified under the Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) programme, ensuring responsibly managed forestry at every step.

**Export readiness:** Indian packaging exporters have decades of experience meeting international shipping standards, including ISTA testing protocols and FEFCO design guidelines used across EU and UK markets.

## Types of Custom Printing for Corrugated Boxes

Understanding the printing options helps you make the right choice for your brand requirements.

### 1. Flexographic Printing

Flexo is the most common method used for corrugated boxes in India. It uses flexible rubber or polymer plates that transfer ink directly to the corrugated surface. Flexo is ideal for:

- Large print runs (typically 500+ units)
- Bold, graphic designs with flat colour areas
- Brand logos, handling instructions, and product identification text
- One to four colour printing at lower cost per unit

Flexo inks used by responsible manufacturers are water-based and low-VOC, reducing environmental impact compared to solvent-based alternatives.

### 2. Offset Lithographic Printing (Litho-Laminate)

For premium unboxing experiences, litho-laminated corrugated boxes involve printing a high-resolution sheet on a litho press and then laminating it onto the corrugated board. This method delivers:

- Photographic-quality images and fine detail
- Full CMYK and Pantone colour matching
- Glossy, matte, or soft-touch laminate finishes
- Ideal for retail-ready or subscription box packaging

The additional lamination layer adds a degree of moisture resistance, extending box life during transit.

### 3. Digital Printing

Digital printing on corrugated is a newer option gaining traction for short runs and personalised packaging. It requires no plate setup, making it cost-effective for:

- Small order quantities (50 to 300 units)
- Seasonal or limited-edition packaging
- Personalisation and variable data printing
- Rapid prototyping before a full production run

## Key Specifications to Define Before Requesting a Quote

Providing precise specifications upfront saves time and ensures accurate quotes from Indian suppliers.

### Box Style

The most common styles for e-commerce use are:

- **RSC (Regular Slotted Container):** The standard shipping box, cost-effective and widely used.
- **FEFCO 0201:** Equivalent to RSC, the European standard designation.
- **Mailer boxes (FEFCO 0330):** Tuck-top, snap-lock style popular for subscription boxes and retail gifting.
- **Telescopic boxes:** Two-piece design offering extra depth, suitable for clothing, footwear, and fragile items.
- **Die-cut boxes:** Custom shapes for retail shelf display or unusual product dimensions.

### Board Grade

The board grade determines strength and weight:

- **Single wall (3-ply):** 3mm to 4mm thick, suitable for lightweight items up to 15 kg.
- **Double wall (5-ply):** 6mm to 7mm thick, for heavier or fragile items requiring extra compression strength.
- **Triple wall (7-ply):** Industrial-grade for very heavy exports.

Common flute profiles in India:
- **B flute:** Thinner, good for retail packaging and short-run die-cuts.
- **C flute:** The most widely used for general shipping boxes.
- **E flute:** Very thin, excellent for close-tolerance printing.
- **BC flute (double wall):** B plus C layers combined for maximum strength.

### Internal Dimensions

Always specify internal dimensions (length x width x height in mm) rather than external. The manufacturer adjusts for board caliper automatically.

### Print Colours and Artwork

Specify Pantone references for brand colours. If artwork is not available in print-ready format (300 DPI, CMYK, PDF or AI), most Indian manufacturers offer design support at nominal cost.

## Eco-Friendly Options to Look For

Sustainable packaging is no longer optional for brands selling to EU and UK markets. Consumer expectations and incoming regulation - including the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation - are pushing brands toward verifiably sustainable packaging.

When sourcing from India, look for these certifications and practices:

**FSC Certification:** The manufacturer sources board from FSC-certified supply chains. This is independently audited and provides chain-of-custody documentation you can share with your customers.

**Recycled Content:** Ask for the percentage of post-consumer recycled (PCR) content in the kraft liner. Many Indian mills produce board with 70% to 100% recycled fibre.

**Water-Based Inks:** Confirm that printing inks are water-based, not solvent-based. Water-based inks are safer, lower in VOCs, and do not interfere with recyclability.

**Biodegradable Coatings:** If you need moisture resistance, ask for water-based barrier coatings rather than polyethylene (PE) laminate. PE-laminated board is not recyclable.

**No Bleach:** Unbleached kraft board (brown, not white) skips the chemical bleaching process, reducing water use and chemical effluent at the mill.

## Quality Standards and Testing

Before approving a new supplier, ask for evidence of quality testing. Standard tests for corrugated boxes include:

- **Edge Crush Test (ECT):** Measures stacking strength of the corrugated board. Expressed in kN/m. Higher ECT ratings are needed for taller palletised stacks.
- **Burst Strength (Mullen Test):** Measures resistance to puncture and bursting. Expressed in kPa or lbf. Relevant for rough handling environments.
- **Box Compression Test (BCT):** Tests the actual compression strength of the finished box, accounting for design and manufacturing quality.
- **Drop Test (ISTA 1A/2A):** Simulates rough handling during transit. ISTA 2A is widely required by international retailers.
- **Humidity Test:** Important for shipments moving through tropical climates or stored in humid warehouses.

Ask your Indian supplier for test reports from an accredited third-party lab. Reputable manufacturers test each batch and will share results.

## Lead Times and Shipping

Typical production lead times for custom printed corrugated boxes from India:

- **Standard orders (1,000 to 10,000 units):** 15 to 25 working days from artwork approval.
- **Large orders (10,000+ units):** 25 to 40 working days depending on complexity.
- **Rush orders:** Some manufacturers accommodate 10 to 15 day production on simple designs for a premium.

Sea freight from major Indian ports (JNPT Mumbai, Mundra, Chennai) to UK ports takes 18 to 28 days. Air freight is 3 to 5 days but economically viable only for small, high-value orders.

Plan for a total lead time of 6 to 10 weeks from order placement to arrival at your warehouse. Buffer for customs clearance and inspection on first imports.

## How to Evaluate an Indian Corrugated Box Manufacturer

Here is a quick checklist for vetting a potential supplier:

**1. Factory audit or third-party inspection:** Request a factory visit, or hire an inspection agency like SGS or Bureau Veritas for a pre-shipment inspection.

**2. Sample order:** Always request printed samples before placing a production order. This reveals print registration accuracy, board quality, and dimensional consistency.

**3. Certifications:** Verify FSC, ISO 9001, and any industry-specific certifications relevant to your market.

**4. References:** Ask for references from customers in your region (UK, USA, EU). A supplier experienced with export compliance reduces risk considerably.

**5. Payment terms:** Standard export terms from India are 30% advance with purchase order, 70% against Bill of Lading. Letter of Credit is an option for very large orders.

**6. Communication responsiveness:** A supplier that responds within 24 hours during business hours (IST, UTC+5:30) and assigns a dedicated account manager is far easier to work with over the long term.
