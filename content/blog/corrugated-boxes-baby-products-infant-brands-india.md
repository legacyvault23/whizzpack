Baby products occupy a unique position in packaging. Unlike most consumer goods categories, the end user cannot advocate for themselves. Parents and caregivers make buying decisions partly on the basis of how a brand presents itself, and that presentation includes what the product arrives in. For UK and US baby brands sourcing corrugated boxes from India, the specification conversation starts with safety, moves through sustainability, and lands on the shelf or the Amazon FBA receiving dock.

This guide walks through every variable that matters, from choosing the right board grade for your specific product category to certifying inks that will pass a restricted substances audit.

## Why Baby Brands Have the Highest Packaging Standards

Retailers in the UK and US impose stricter packaging requirements on baby products than on almost any other category. Amazon's baby product policy layers suffocation warnings, age-grading labels, and category-specific drop tests on top of its standard FBA requirements. High Street retailers and independent baby boutiques often require FSC certification as a baseline for shelf eligibility. And parents themselves are increasingly scrutinising every element of what a product touches.

India can supply corrugated boxes that meet all of these requirements, often at a significant cost advantage over European or North American alternatives. The key is building the right specification from the outset rather than trying to retrofit compliance after production.

## Board Grades by Baby Product Category

Choosing the correct board grade depends on the weight and fragility of what goes inside, plus the stacking and transit load the box will face.

### Soft Goods: Clothing, Bibs, and Muslin Wraps

Lightweight baby clothing and textile accessories rarely exceed 2 kg per unit. For this category, E-flute single-wall board is the standard choice. It packs flat for efficient sea freight, provides enough rigidity to protect the brand presentation on retail shelves, and takes printed artwork cleanly. If the box is going direct-to-consumer rather than to retail, B-flute single-wall gives a slightly sturdier feel for DTC unboxing.

### Feeding and Bath: Bottles, Cups, and Bath Accessories

Products in this category sit in the medium-weight bracket. A single-wall C-flute or B-flute box handles most feeding bottle sets comfortably. Where bottles or jars are glass rather than plastic, cell dividers (also known as partitions or layer pads) are essential. These are pre-cut corrugated inserts that separate each unit inside the master carton, preventing glass-on-glass contact during transit. Whizzpack produces cell dividers to custom configurations, specified by cell count per layer and cell dimension.

### Heavy Nursery Gear: Bouncers, Rockers, and Play Mats

Nursery equipment that ships fully assembled or in large component sets typically requires double-wall BC-flute board. The BC combination gives higher edge crush strength for the vertical stacking loads that occur in a container or a fulfilment warehouse. For play mats and larger items, foam corner inserts alongside the double-wall outer provide an additional layer of protection that also communicates product quality at the unboxing stage.

### Formula and Nutritional Products

Baby formula and supplementary nutrition products that are packaged in a corrugated secondary or tertiary box require food-contact-safe board. This means the inner liner must be virgin kraft, not recycled, and inks must comply with applicable indirect food contact standards. If you are unsure whether your product falls into this category, your regulatory team will advise; the packaging requirement follows the product's regulatory classification.

## Non-Toxic Inks and Safety Standards

For baby and infant brands, ink safety is not optional. The question is which standard applies to your specific product and market.

### Water-Based and Soy-Based Flexographic Inks

Standard commercial inks used in corrugated printing contain mineral oil hydrocarbons (MOAH and MOSH compounds) that can migrate into food or onto surfaces that infants touch. Whizzpack's default for baby brand orders is water-based flexographic ink, which eliminates the MOAH/MOSH concern and meets the requirements of the Nestlé Guidance on Packaging Inks (a widely used industry benchmark for indirect food contact).

Soy-based inks are an alternative that many baby brands specify for their lower environmental impact. They achieve comparable colour saturation to conventional inks on recycled board and carry a lower volatile organic compound (VOC) emission profile.

### EN 71 Considerations

EN 71 is the European toy safety standard, and it includes limits on specific elements in materials that toys and toy packaging are made from. If your product includes items that meet the EN 71 definition of a toy (which captures many baby rattles, teethers, and activity products), the packaging that ships with or accompanies those products should be evaluated for compliance. Your certification body will advise on scope; we can supply board and ink specifications to support that evaluation.

## FSC Certification and Eco-Conscious Parent Brands

![Baby shoes in pastel-coloured branded gift boxes showing how infant brands use premium corrugated packaging for retail presentation](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2025/02/17/17/37/baby-shoes-boxes-9413528_1280.jpg)

FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) certification is the most widely recognised sustainable forest management standard in the UK and US baby market. The core claim for most brands is FSC Mix, which allows the box to carry the FSC Mix label provided a defined percentage of the board fibre comes from FSC-certified sources.

For baby brands specifically, FSC serves a dual function. It is a retail listing requirement for a growing number of UK and US mass-market retailers. It is also a brand positioning tool. Parents are among the most environmentally engaged consumer segments, and FSC-labelled packaging signals that the brand takes its values seriously beyond the product itself.

Whizzpack holds FSC chain-of-custody certification. All corrugated boxes ordered under FSC Mix or FSC 100% schemes are produced using certified board sourced through audited supply chains, with the FSC logo printed on the box as part of the artwork.

### OEKO-TEX for Inner Packaging Materials

Where a corrugated outer box contains tissue paper, kraft paper wrap, or fabric inserts (common for premium baby clothing brands), the OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification provides assurance that those materials have been tested for harmful substances. OEKO-TEX Standard 100 covers a broader range of substances than FSC, including allergenic dyes, formaldehyde, and heavy metals. If your brand already uses OEKO-TEX certified cotton bags or muslin packaging, extending that certification to any paper-based inner packaging elements is a logical next step.

## Amazon FBA Requirements for Baby Products

Amazon Baby is one of the most compliance-intensive categories in the FBA programme. The requirements below are consistent across the UK (amazon.co.uk) and US (amazon.com) marketplaces, though sellers should verify current policy in Seller Central before confirming a final specification.

![Baby in a bathrobe with Johnson's baby care products illustrating the range of infant care items that require correctly specified corrugated secondary packaging](https://images.pexels.com/photos/20509001/pexels-photo-20509001.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1200)

### Suffocation Warning Labels

Any product that ships with a poly bag (including clear film wrapping inside the corrugated box) requires a suffocation warning printed directly on the bag in a minimum font size specified by Amazon. This is a product-level requirement rather than a box-level requirement, but the corrugated packaging specification needs to accommodate the labelled inner packs.

### Drop Test Standards

Amazon requires that products in the Baby category survive a minimum series of drop tests without damage to the product. The drop test parameters depend on the product weight and dimensions. For many baby products, a 1.2-metre drop test from multiple orientations is the baseline. Double-wall BC-flute or reinforced single-wall C-flute with corner protection typically achieves this. We can arrange third-party drop testing at an accredited laboratory in India prior to your first production run.

### FNSKU Labelling

The Amazon FNSKU barcode must be scannable on the outer carton at the receiving dock. Standard box artwork should include a designated blank zone for FNSKU application. Whizzpack can pre-print the FNSKU directly on the box if you are producing a single SKU with a confirmed ASIN, which eliminates the application step at a 3PL.

### Oversized Items

Products that ship in an oversized outer box (defined by Amazon as any unit with the longest side exceeding a certain threshold, currently 457mm in the US and 462mm in the UK) require additional labelling and may incur different FBA fees. Bouncers, play mats, and assembled nursery furniture typically fall into this category. We supply double-wall boxes in custom dimensions for oversized FBA shipments.

## Custom Print for Infant Brand Identity

Baby product packaging is one of the highest-investment categories for custom print, because brand differentiation at the shelf or the unboxing moment drives meaningful commercial outcomes. Parents who have a positive unboxing experience are significantly more likely to repurchase and recommend.

### Colour Palette and Print Process

Soft pastel palettes (sage green, blush pink, sky blue, warm cream) are dominant in the premium baby category, particularly for UK boutique and DTC brands. These translate well to 2-colour or 3-colour flexographic print on natural kraft board, where the natural board colour becomes part of the visual design rather than something to cover.

For brands with complex artwork, full-colour flexographic print (up to 6 colours) is available on white-top kraft liner. The white top gives a cleaner base for precise colour matching, which matters for brands with rigidly defined brand guidelines.

### Matte Laminate and Tactile Finishes

Matte laminate applied to the outer surface of the corrugated box gives a soft, premium feel that is associated with higher quality. It is widely used by UK baby boutique brands and is compatible with FSC certification provided the laminate itself meets relevant certification requirements. Note that laminate does affect recyclability; brands that prioritise end-of-life recycling may prefer uncoated natural kraft with a water-based varnish finish instead.

### Cell Dividers for Glass Bottles

If your product range includes glass nursing bottles, glass feeding jars, or glass storage containers, cell dividers are a critical component of the box specification. We produce corrugated cell dividers to match your exact bottle diameter and height, with a minimum cell depth that ensures the glass top sits below the top of the master carton when the lid is closed.

## UK and US Import Considerations

Corrugated boxes imported from India into the UK are classified under HS code 4819.10 (cartons, boxes, and cases of corrugated paper or paperboard). The current UK Global Tariff rate is 0% for this code, with no anti-dumping duty applicable to Indian-origin goods.

For US imports, the applicable HTS code is 4819.10.0040. India is not subject to the Section 301 tariffs that apply to Chinese corrugated packaging, which is a material cost advantage for US buyers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is FSC certification included as standard, or is it an add-on?**

FSC Mix certification is available as a standard option on corrugated box orders. It requires that you specify the FSC claim at the time of ordering so that we can source the correct certified board. There is no additional charge for the FSC certification itself, though FSC-certified board can carry a small premium over non-certified equivalents depending on market conditions.

**Can you match Pantone colours for brand consistency?**

Yes. We work from Pantone Matching System (PMS) references and produce a press-approved proof before the main production run. Final colour on kraft board will differ slightly from the colour on a white substrate, so we always provide a physical sample for approval before confirming.

**Do you supply just the boxes, or also the cell dividers and inserts?**

We supply the outer corrugated box, cell dividers, foam corner inserts, and layer pads as a complete set. All components are produced in-house and shipped as a single consignment, which simplifies freight and customs documentation.

**What information do you need to produce a quotation?**

We need: outer box dimensions (length x width x depth in mm), board grade and flute, print specification (number of colours, artwork file or brief), cell divider configuration if applicable, and destination port. With this information, we can typically return a detailed quotation within 2 to 3 working days.

**Are your boxes tested for drop performance before shipment?**

Quality inspection is conducted on every production run. Drop testing to Amazon FBA or EN 71 standards can be arranged at an accredited third-party laboratory prior to dispatch, with a testing certificate provided. This service is available on request and is recommended for brands placing their first order.
