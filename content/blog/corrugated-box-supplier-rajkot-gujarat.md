## Why Gujarat Dominates Indian Corrugated Box Manufacturing

Gujarat accounts for a disproportionate share of India's corrugated box export capacity. Several factors explain this:

**Raw material proximity:** Gujarat is one of India's largest cotton-growing states and a major hub for paper and board processing. Proximity to raw materials reduces input logistics costs for manufacturers.

**Industrial infrastructure:** Gujarat's manufacturing industry - spanning textiles, chemicals, engineering, and packaging - has built up decades of factory infrastructure, skilled labour, and export logistics capability. The Kandla Special Economic Zone (SEZ) and Mundra Port provide efficient export facilities.

**Mundra Port access:** Mundra Port (operated by Adani Ports) is India's largest private container port and handles a significant share of India's packaging exports. Rajkot-based factories load containers at Mundra in approximately 4 to 6 hours of road transport. This gives Gujarat manufacturers a logistics advantage over factories further from the port.

**Concentration of buyers:** Over decades, Gujarat packaging manufacturers have built direct relationships with US and UK importers. This accumulated export experience - understanding of international documentation, quality certification systems, and buyer expectations - makes Gujarat factories more reliably export-ready than equivalent facilities in states with less export history.

**Cluster advantages:** When packaging manufacturers concentrate geographically, they share a pool of skilled workers (corrugating machine operators, print technicians, quality inspectors), trained to international standards. This cluster effect raises the general quality floor.

## What Rajkot Corrugated Box Manufacturers Produce

Rajkot-based corrugated manufacturers produce the full range of standard corrugated box formats for export:

**RSC (Regular Slotted Carton):** The most common export corrugated box. Produced in single wall (3-ply) and double wall (5-ply) in all standard flute types (B, C, BC). Any custom dimension at MOQ 5,000 units.

**Die-cut boxes:** Custom shapes, tray boxes, retail display boxes, and self-locking bottom configurations. Require a tooling setup (cutting die) with lead time and cost, but production unit cost is equivalent to RSC at volume.

**Heavy-duty boxes:** Double wall and triple wall corrugated for industrial goods, machinery components, and products requiring BCT (Box Compression Test) ratings above standard. Gujarat manufacturers are among the most experienced in India for heavy-duty export specifications.

**Custom printed boxes:** 1 to 4 colour flexographic printing on corrugated. White-top liner available for higher print quality. Full-colour offset litho laminate for premium branded boxes.

**Mailer boxes:** E-flute and B-flute mailers for e-commerce and subscription box brands. Custom dimensions, full-colour printing inside and outside.

## Port Logistics from Rajkot

**Road to Mundra:** Rajkot to Mundra Port is approximately 130km by NH-27, typically 3 to 4 hours driving time. Factory-to-port logistics are handled by the factory's freight agent or your appointed freight forwarder.

**Container stuffing:** Most Rajkot factories stuff containers at the factory gate under customs supervision (factory stuffing under Export General Manifest). This is more efficient than CFS (Container Freight Station) stuffing and gives the buyer visibility into the loading process.

**Documentation at Mundra:** The full export documentation set (commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, Certificate of Origin, quality test certificates) is prepared by the factory's export department and the customs broker. A Gujarat-based manufacturer with active US and UK export relationships will have a documented process for this.
