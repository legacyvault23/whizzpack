FAQ

Common Questions

What sizes of pizza boxes do you make?
We manufacture pizza boxes from 7-inch to 16-inch and larger on custom order. All boxes are made to your exact L x W x H specification with in-house cutting dies. See the size reference table above for standard dimensions and flute options.
Are your pizza boxes food-grade?
Yes. All boxes use virgin kraft paper on the food-contact inner surfaces. We do not use recycled board on any surface that touches food. Grease-resistant (GR) coating is available as standard on all our pizza box formats.
What is the MOQ for pizza boxes?
Our minimum order quantity is 5,000 units per SKU (size and design combination). For pizza chains ordering multiple sizes, we can consider a combined MOQ arrangement across SKUs. Contact us to discuss your specific requirements.
How do you handle custom printing?
We offer flexographic printing in 1-4 colours for high-volume pizza box orders - this is the most cost-effective option for chains. Full-colour digital printing is available for shorter pilot runs or seasonal specials. All inks are water-based and food-safe. We can work with your artwork or our design team can create artwork at no charge.
How long does production take?
Standard production is 15-20 working days after artwork approval. Printed samples in your exact dimensions are air-shipped within 5-7 working days. Full production begins only after your written sample approval. We also offer expedited production for urgent requirements - contact us to discuss.
Can I see a sample before ordering?
Yes, always. We produce printed sample boxes in your exact dimensions and ship them by air within 5-7 working days. Full production never begins without your written sample approval. Sample cost is typically nominal and credited back against your first production order.
Do you export pizza boxes internationally?
Yes. We export from our factory in Rajkot, Gujarat to customers in the USA, UK, UAE, Australia and 20+ other countries. We handle all export documentation, customs compliance, and sea or air freight coordination. Typical sea freight transit to UK/USA is 25-35 days.
